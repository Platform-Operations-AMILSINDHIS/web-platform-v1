## <p align="center" style="color: #1F2937;">Community Portal: User Guide</p>

---

### 🌟 Welcome to the Amil Sindhis Portal
This guide will help you navigate the digital home of the Khudabadi Amil Panchayat (KAP) and Young Amil Circle (YAC). Our platform is designed to streamline memberships, matrimony services, and community engagement.

---

### 1. Getting Started: Account Creation
To access the full features of the site, you must first create a general account.

1.  **Sign Up**: Click the **Sign Up** button in the top navigation bar.
2.  **Details**: Provide your basic information:
    *   Full Name, Email, and Phone Number.
    *   **Date of Birth**: This is crucial for membership eligibility (YAC: 16-30, KAP: 21+).
    *   **Gender**: Required for matrimony services.
3.  **Profile Picture**: Upload a clear profile picture. This will be your primary identity on the portal.
4.  **Verification**: Check your email for an activation link to verify your account.

---

### 2. Membership Applications
Once logged in, you can apply for community memberships.

#### **Young Amil Circle (YAC)**
*   **Eligibility**: Ages 16 to 30.
*   **Process**: Navigate to the YAC page, fill in your personal, family, and address details.
*   **Payment**: Scan the provided QR code and enter the Transaction/Payment ID in the form.
*   **Consent**: Provide consent for photo usage if you intend to use matrimony services.

#### **Khudabadi Amil Panchayat (KAP)**
*   **Eligibility**: Age 21 and above.
*   **Process**: Similar to YAC, fill in the comprehensive multi-step form.
*   **Existing Members**: If you are already a member of the physical Panchayat, check the "Existing Member" box to migrate your record to the digital portal.

---

### 3. Matrimony Services
The Matrimony section is a private, secure area for members looking for life partners.

1.  **Access**: You must be an approved KAP or YAC member to access Matrimony.
2.  **Build Profile**: Fill out the detailed Matrimony Form, including:
    *   Personal & Professional background.
    *   Spouse Preferences (Height, Education, etc.).
    *   Horoscope details (Manglik status).
3.  **Photos**: You can upload up to **3 high-quality photos** specifically for your matrimony profile.
4.  **Browsing**: Once approved, you can view other profiles.
5.  **Requesting Profiles**: If you find a match, click **"Request Profile"**. The admin office will review your request and, if approved, send the full profile PDF to your registered email.

---

### 4. Managing Your Profile
You can update your details at any time:
1.  Click on your name in the top right corner and select **"My Profile"**.
2.  Click **"Edit Profile"** to update your name, gender, or date of birth.
3.  Click **"Edit Profile Picture"** to refresh your avatar.
4.  **Note**: Your email address is locked to your account and cannot be changed for security reasons.
